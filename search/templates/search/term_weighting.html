Method details

Raw TF · TF(t,d) = count(t,d).

Log TF · 1 + ln(TF), TF > 0; otherwise 0.

IDF · ln(N / DF(t)), N = indexed document count.

TF-IDF · Raw TF × IDF.

cosine · (q · d) / (||q||₂ ||d||₂).

L2 · Unit vectors plus dot product give the same cosine ranking.
