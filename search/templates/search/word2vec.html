What is being learned?

Skip-gram predicts neighboring tokens from a center token; CBOW predicts a center token from its neighbors. Gensim trains weights with negative sampling. B keeps stopwords, C removes the shared 72 stopwords, and D also uses Porter stems. No sentence crosses an abstract boundary.

Small scientific collections may produce unstable neighbors. The same seed, stable hash and single worker support repeatability in the same software environment; they do not make the embeddings ground truth.
