How this analysis is calculated

Only HW1 extracted Abstract + Keywords are analyzed. Titles, full text, reference lists and structured-abstract headings are excluded. All four conditions use the same distinct documents.

A includes word tokens and standalone punctuation marks. B removes standalone punctuation while preserving biomedical connectors, apostrophes, decimal points and percentages inside terms. C additionally removes the shared stopword list. D applies the NLTK PorterStemmer to C.

Terms are ordered by descending CF, breaking ties alphabetically. Ordinary least squares fits ln(CF) against ln(rank) over every vocabulary entry; exponent k is the negative slope. Charts use a subset of log-spaced display points for performance; calculations and exports use every term.

The export contains all four full vocabularies, summary metrics, regional fits, and the exact corpus with IDs, abstracts and topic memberships. All measurements come from your current collection.
